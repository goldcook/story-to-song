# Third-party audio notices

## tonejs-instruments samples

The piano, acoustic-guitar, cello, and xylophone MP3 samples under
`public/audio/instruments/` come from the
[tonejs-instruments](https://github.com/nbrosowsky/tonejs-instruments) sample
library by Nicholaus P. Brosowsky, pinned to upstream commit
`622c2f1c32c8cfce4158ddc3eb26e518ddef37e5`.

The upstream project releases its samples under the
[Creative Commons Attribution 3.0 license](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/).
The files are redistributed here without audio modification. Only a small
multisample subset is included so the static site remains practical to load.
The collection's `sample-source-info.txt` identifies the earlier sources used
for these instruments as VSCO 2 Community Edition / Versilian Studios (piano
and xylophone), the University of Iowa Musical Instrument Samples (acoustic
guitar), and Freesound user `flcellogrl`, recording `12408__flcellogrl__real-cello-notes`
(cello).

## Versilian Community Sample Library

The kick, shaker, and woodblock WAV samples under `public/audio/percussion/`
come from the [Versilian Community Sample Library](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL)
at commit `c1ea7bcc3c7309650ab0da9d15c9cd1fbc4a4c7e`.

VCSL is released under
[CC0 1.0 Universal](https://creativecommons.org/publicdomain/zero/1.0/).
The files are redistributed here without audio modification.
